# PropTech Marketing & Lead Generation Strategy (`/marketing-strategy`)

Use this skill to systematically build an organic and outbound pipeline of real estate builder leads without burning cash on Google Ads.

---

## 1. The RERA Registration Lead-Gen Pipeline (Zero-Cost Outbound)

Every state RERA portal in India (K-RERA for Karnataka, MahaRERA for Maharashtra, TSRERA for Telangana) publishes public records of every newly approved residential project every month.

### The Playbook:
1. **Scrape or Download Monthly RERA Grants:**
   - Filter for projects with **30 to 180 units** approved in the last 6 months.
   - Extract: Promoter Company Name, Registered Office Address, Promoter Phone/Email, Project Architect, and RERA Project Manager.
2. **Timing the Outreach:**
   - Projects 3–6 months post-RERA approval are actively entering their excavation / basement foundation stage.
   - This is the exact moment they start selling units and allotting basement parking slots.
3. **Outbound Channel:**
   - Send targeted WhatsApp messages directly to the Promoter / RERA Authorized Signatory introducing DeedFlow.

## 2. Inbound Content & LinkedIn Authority Playbook

Target Audience: Head of Post-Sales, CRM Directors, Real Estate Legal Heads.

### Core Content Pillars:
1. **"The Cost of a Stalled Home Loan Disbursement"**
   - Break down how a ₹50 Lakh tranche delayed by 18 days due to an improperly formatted Builder NOC costs the developer ₹22,000 in working capital overdraft interest.
2. **"The Nightmare of Basement Parking Clashes"**
   - Real-world case studies of buyer disputes caused by dual allotment of tandem car parking slots and how visual inventory grids solve it.
3. **"Kaveri 2.0 & Digital Deed Automation in Bengaluru"**
   - Practical walkthroughs for builders and property advocates on streamlining pre-registration data entry.

## 3. Institutional & Ecosystem Alliances

1. **CREDAI & NAREDCO Youth Wings:**
   - The 2nd-generation family members in Indian real estate developer families (usually educated abroad, tech-friendly, aged 25–35) hate outdated manual paperwork.
   - Sponsor or present a 10-minute demo at CREDAI Youth Wing monthly breakfasts: *"How Modern Builders Automate Post-Sales Documentation."*
2. **Empanelled Bank Home Loan DSA (Direct Selling Agents):**
   - DSAs for HDFC, SBI, and ICICI are incentivized on loan disbursement volume.
   - If a builder’s paperwork is slow, the DSA doesn't get their commission.
   - Give DSAs a free account to generate verified builder kits for their clients.
3. **Sub-Registrar Legal Advocates:**
   - Partner with local advocates handling builder project registrations.
   - Offer them a co-branded portal to manage client boundary schedules and agreement drafts.
