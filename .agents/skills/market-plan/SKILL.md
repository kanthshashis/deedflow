# Indian PropTech Go-To-Market & Market Strategy (`/market-plan`)

Use this skill to guide market entry, segment targeting, competitive positioning, and geography rollout.

## 1. Market Sizing (India Real Estate Document & Post-Sales Tech)

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** ~150,000 active RERA-registered residential & commercial projects across India.
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** ~35,000 mid-sized builders (projects with 25–250 units) across top 8 real estate hubs:
  1. Bengaluru (Karnataka) - Primary Beachhead
  2. MMR & Pune (Maharashtra) - Secondary High-Value
  3. Hyderabad (Telangana)
  4. NCR (Gurugram/Noida)
  5. Chennai (Tamil Nadu)
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM - Years 1-2):** 250 active builders in Bengaluru / Karnataka (generating ~15,000 document packs/year).

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Segmentation

| Tier | Builder Type | Unit Volume | Current Tool | Pain Level | Willingness to Pay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A (Sweet Spot)** | Standalone Mid-Sized Developers (e.g. 1–3 ongoing projects, 50–150 flats each) | 50–300 flats/yr | MS Word + Google Sheets | High (Small post-sales team drowning in paperwork) | ₹2,999 – ₹7,999/mo |
| **Tier B** | High-Volume Independent Channel Partners / Master Brokers (Anarock, Square Yards, regional IPCs) | 200–1,000 bookings/yr | CRM or paper files | Very High (Need instant spot-booking dockets) | ₹500/booking or ₹15,000/mo |
| **Tier C (Avoid Early)** | Tier-1 Conglomerates (Prestige, Sobha, Brigade, Godrej) | 2,000+ units/yr | SAP, Farvision, Salesforce | Low urgency (Have 50-person legal departments; 12-month procurement cycles) | High budget, but impossible to close for a seed startup |
| **Tier D** | SRO Registration Advocates & Deed Writers | 10–50 deeds/mo | Pirated Word 2007, PageMaker | Medium (Need speed, but hate paying SaaS) | ₹100–₹250 per deed generated |

## 4. Rollout Strategy: The 90-Day Bengaluru Beachhead
- **Month 1 (Validation & Pilots):** 5 pilot builders in East/South Bengaluru (Whitefield, Sarjapur, Electronic City). Zero-cost beta usage for their ongoing towers.
- **Month 2 (Iteration & Case Studies):** Gather metrics: *"Saved 42 hours across 80 bookings at Project X; zero loan rejections from SBI."*
- **Month 3 (Commercial Rollout):** Convert 3 pilots to paid annual retainers; launch WhatsApp self-serve onboarding.
