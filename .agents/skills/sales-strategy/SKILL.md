# Field Sales Playbook: Indian Builders & Site Offices (`/sales-strategy`)

Use this skill to navigate in-person site visits, pitch post-sales heads and promoters, neutralize objections, and close zero-friction pilot agreements.

---

## 1. The Site Visit Protocol (How to Get Past the Guard to the Decision Maker)

1. **Target Timing:** Tuesday to Thursday between **11:30 AM and 2:30 PM**.  
   *(Never visit on Saturday/Sunday when sales reps are busy with walk-in buyers; never visit on Monday morning when they have weekly review meetings).*
2. **Who to Ask For:** Do not ask for the "Managing Director" (you will be blocked). Ask for:  
   - *"Post-Sales Manager"* or  
   - *"CRM Head / Customer Relations Manager"* or  
   - *"Bank Home Loan Coordinator"*
3. **The Entrance Hook:**  
   *"Namaste, I’m [Name]. I work with PropTech automation for builders in Bangalore. I don't need more than 5 minutes—I just wanted to show you a 30-second tool that prepares your Bank Home Loan NOCs and Car Parking letters without your team having to type them into Word."*

---

## 2. The 3-Minute Live Mobile Demo Script

- **Step 1 (Physical Anchor):** Pull out a physical sample PAN card (or ask them for any dummy business card/ID).
- **Step 2 (The Magic):** Open DeedFlow on your phone. Snap a photo.
- **Step 3 (The Reveal):** Within 10 seconds, show the phone screen:
  - *"See, it read the name, masked the ID for privacy, picked flat 302 from the dropdown, checked that slot B1-14 is free, and already generated both the Word file and this watermarked draft."*
- **Step 4 (The WhatsApp Close):** Tap "Share via WhatsApp" and send the draft PDF link to their phone number on the spot.
- **Step 5 (Silence):** Let them open it on their own phone. Watch their reaction.

## 3. Objection Handling Battlecard

### Objection 1: *"We already use an Excel sheet and MS Word. It costs us zero rupees."*
- **Response:** *"Absolutely, Word is great. But how many times has an executive accidentally left the previous buyer's name or wrong flat number on page 2 of a Word file? When SBI bank's advocate catches that, the customer's loan gets held up by 2 weeks. DeedFlow guarantees zero copy-paste typos and gives you the filled Word file in 15 seconds."*

### Objection 2: *"We use Farvision / Highbar / Salesforce."*
- **Response:** *"Farvision is your master ERP for accounting and billing—you shouldn't replace it. But does your sales rep at the site office generate instant Bank Kits and WhatsApp review links right from their phone on a Sunday afternoon in Farvision? DeedFlow is the quick lightweight tool for your post-sales desk at the project site."*

### Objection 3: *"Every bank (SBI, HDFC, Canara) has their own format. A standard template won't work."*
- **Response:** *"That is exactly why we built DeedFlow with custom template upload. Give me your exact SBI NOC Word file right now. I'll upload it into your private dashboard in 60 seconds, and it will fill your exact bank format every single time."*

## 4. The "Zero-Risk Pilot Agreement"
Never push for an annual SaaS contract on day 1. Offer the **30-Day Project Pilot**:
- Setup time: 10 minutes (import their flat inventory CSV).
- Cost: ₹0 for the first 30 days or first 25 bookings.
- Success Metric: If it saves their post-sales clerk 10+ hours and eliminates loan documentation errors, they transition to a flat ₹25,000/year license per ongoing tower.
