# WhatsApp Growth & Outreach Funnel Playbook (`/whatsapp-growth`)

In Indian real estate, **nobody checks email**. Deals are closed, documents are circulated, and payments are tracked 100% over **WhatsApp**. Use this skill for cold outreach, buyer draft verification, and referral generation.

---

## 1. Direct Cold Outreach to Post-Sales Heads & Promoters

### Script 1: Cold Message to Post-Sales / CRM Manager (High Response Rate)
> *Namaste [Name] ji! Saw your ongoing tower at [Project Name] in [Location]. Quick question: How much time does your team spend manually typing buyer PAN details into Word for Car Parking Allotments and Bank Home Loan NOCs?*  
>  
> *We built a simple 15-second tool: you snap a phone photo of the buyer’s PAN card, and it instantly fills your exact company letterhead + Bank NOC + Cost Sheet without typos.*  
>  
> *Here is a 30-second screen recording of it working for a Bangalore builder: [Video Link]*  
>  
> *Can I set it up for your [Project Name] for free this week? You can test it on 5 bookings and see if it saves your team time.*

## 2. The In-App "1-Click WhatsApp Draft Verification" Flow
When a sales clerk generates a draft in DeedFlow, the app produces a 1-click WhatsApp link for the customer.

### Template Sent to Home Buyer for Approval:
> *Dear [Mr./Ms. BuyerName],*  
>  
> *Congratulations on your booking of Flat No. [FlatNo] at [ProjectName]!*  
>  
> *To ensure 100% zero spelling mistakes in your upcoming Sale Agreement and Bank Home Loan dossier, please review your official draft details here (takes 30 seconds):*  
> 👉 *https://deedflow.in/verify/[DocumentHash]*  
>  
> *Details on file:*  
> • *Allottee: [BuyerName]*  
> • *Flat No: [FlatNo] (Tower [Tower])*  
> • *Allocated Parking Bay: [SlotNo] ([BasementLevel])*  
> • *Super Built-Up Area: [SBA] sq.ft*  
>  
> *Please reply with "CONFIRMED" or let us know if any name spelling needs correction.*  
> *— Post-Sales Team, [BuilderName]*

## 3. The "Stalled Bank Loan" Panic Outreach (For Loan Coordinators)
> *Hi [Name], are you facing delays in getting home loan tranches disbursed from SBI or HDFC for buyers at [Project Name]?*  
>  
> *Most bank legal teams reject files due to small mismatches in the Tripartite NOC or Demand Notice schedule. We have pre-approved, bank-tested templates that pass SBI/HDFC legal verification on the first attempt.*  
>  
> *Happy to share the SBI format with you directly on WhatsApp. Let me know!*

---

## 4. Automation Rules
- Always include the builder’s exact project name and location (never send generic spam).
- Always attach or link a short 15-second Loom/GIF demonstrating the mobile phone photo $\to$ filled `.docx` flow.
- Follow up within 48 hours with a single short text: *"Should I keep this active for your site office this weekend?"*
